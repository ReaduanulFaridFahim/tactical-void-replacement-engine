📐 Methodology & Algorithmic Architecture

Traditional football scouting relies on subjective scout reports or static FIFA ratings. The Tactical Void Engine operates on two novel mathematical layers:

1. Tactical Void Quantification

Measures the difference in club points-per-game, goal creation, and defensive collapse across an empirical 25-game window pre- and post-departure.

Void Index = 50 - 22(ΔPPG) - 0.35(ΔWin%) - 15(ΔGF) + 12(ΔGA)

2. Cosine Vector Embeddings

Encodes player performance into normalized per-90 vectors covering goal creation, playmaking, minute durability, and physical profile.

CosSim(A, B) = (A · B) / (||A|| · ||B||)

3. Transfer Arbitrage Optimization

Calculates financial efficiency by identifying candidates who deliver 90%+ tactical compatibility while costing only a fraction of the liquidated fee.

Savings = Fee_Received - Candidate_Valuation
